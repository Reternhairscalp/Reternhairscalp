# Purpose

This document defines the complete customer journey from the first interaction with Rettern Hair Growth until the customer becomes a loyal lifetime customer.

Every department, SOP, AI Agent, and automation inside RIP must follow this customer journey.

---

# Customer Journey Philosophy

Every customer should experience a smooth, personalised and measurable journey.

The objective is not only to sell treatments, but to build long-term relationships.
