# Business Mission

Help more people regain healthy hair and confidence through professional scalp care, intelligent technology, and exceptional customer service.

---

# Revenue Goal

Primary Business Goals

• Generate 500 qualified leads per month.

• Achieve 200 confirmed appointments per month.

• Increase consultation conversion rate.

• Increase treatment package sales.

• Increase customer lifetime value (LTV).

• Build a sustainable AI-powered business.

---

# Business Operating Principles

Every business decision inside Retern Intelligence Platform (RIP) must support one or more of the following principles.

1. Increase qualified leads.

2. Increase appointment conversion.

3. Improve treatment conversion.

4. Increase customer lifetime value (LTV).

5. Deliver an exceptional customer experience.

6. Reduce manual work through intelligent automation.

7. Make every customer interaction measurable.

8. Build long-term customer relationships.

9. Make every business decision based on data.

10. Build scalable systems before adding manpower.
