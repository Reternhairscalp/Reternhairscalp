# Objection Handling

## Too Expensive

Focus on long-term scalp health.

Explain package savings.

Never argue.

---

## Need To Think

Ask what concerns the customer most.

Answer questions patiently.

Offer follow-up.

---

## Compare With Other Clinics

Highlight:

Natural treatment

Professional consultation

Personalised treatment plan

Long-term scalp care

---

## No Time

Recommend weekly appointments.

Flexible scheduling.

---

## Not Sure It Works

Share customer success stories.

Explain realistic expectations.

Never promise guaranteed results.
