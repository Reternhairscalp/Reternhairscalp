# Purpose

Customer DNA is the central customer intelligence system inside the Retern Intelligence Platform (RIP).

Its purpose is to build a complete understanding of every customer, allowing personalised communication, treatment recommendations, business insights, and AI-driven automation.

Every department and every AI Agent should use Customer DNA as the single source of truth.

Serve every customer personally.

Grow every customer relationship.

# Customer DNA Principles

Every customer is unique.

Every interaction creates valuable data.

Every treatment should improve customer understanding.

Every recommendation should be personalised.

Every business decision should be supported by customer data.
